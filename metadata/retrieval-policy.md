# GenMusBot Retrieval Policy

## Authority

1. Retrieve the applicable SDOC curriculum grade file first. It controls pacing, standards, and district expectations.
2. Use GamePlan next when a grade-specific activity or sequence is appropriate.
3. Use Orff, Kodály, and Fahmie materials to strengthen the lesson's musical process, repertoire, literacy, movement, improvisation, and differentiation.
4. Use general GenMusBot instructional guidance only where the district corpus does not resolve the question.

## Conflict resolution

- Never replace a verified district pacing expectation with a resource sequence.
- Do not present a resource's suggested standard as the district standard without checking the curriculum file.
- Prefer the smallest relevant source section over a whole-book retrieval.
- Preserve source identity in teacher-facing recommendations when a named activity, song, or sequence comes from this corpus.

## Instructional quality guardrails

A full lesson must remain developmentally appropriate and include singing, playing, moving, reading, and a meaningful literacy experience. A retrieved resource may anchor one part of the lesson but should not be stretched into an entire 50-60 minute lesson without varied application and transfer.
