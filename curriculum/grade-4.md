Standards/
Benchmarks

Unit Q1 Unit 3: Part-Singing: Descants & Triads
Standards/
Benchmarks
MU.4.S.3.1: Sing rounds, canons, and/or partner songs in an appropriate range, using proper vocal 
technique and maintaining pitch.
