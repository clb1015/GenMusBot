Standards/
Benchmarks

Assessment Strategies
Formative Assessment/Progress Monitoring Summative Assessment
• Students aurally identify the meter (2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 6/8) 
of various listening examples.
• Students echo-clap complex rhythmic phrases in
