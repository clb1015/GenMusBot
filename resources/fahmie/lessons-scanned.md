with the cup. Each.step above produces a different tone color. Give the students the

things before tackling the second phrase.

spot in time to retrieve the next cup. (Or the person at the end can begin stacking and 
the game ends when the,cups run out) This is particularly effective if used in a 
program, because seated in a circle, the audience only sees the backs of the 
performers.

students to really concentrate. At first I vary the tempo in predictable spots. Later I put 
in tempo changes very unexpectedly. To their surprise, the students discover that 
slower is MUCH HARDER than faster once they have the hang of it. 
*Try reversing the direction in which the cup is passed. Cups will move to the left on 
the last beat. Hands will need to reverse on the second phrase above. Once this 
direction is mastered, I use the temple blocks again with changing rhythms and when 
the class hears a loud "BEEP" (ala Marveleen Moore) the direction reverses. This is 
just TOO much fun and quite a challenge.
